# Changelog

All notable public changes to **Open Problems in Mechanics** are recorded here.

The project uses versioned public snapshots while remaining a living resource.

## 11 October 2026 — Eshelby stability preprint and EL01 v1.1

- Linked the Vision essay to Bo Ni's arXiv preprint, [Quantitative Eshelby stability: local and finite-shape theory through AI-assisted mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13400), and added a concise scientific summary and full reference.
- Updated EL01 with nonlinear two-sided local stability, critical half-order geometric scaling and the exact sphere spectrum, and convergent bounds for prescribed finite shapes. Stated the physical assumptions and recorded the independent finite-element comparisons as numerical evidence.
- Distinguished the remaining global geometry-only stability question from the available finite-shape construction, and revised the criteria for further progress.
- Added the preprint reference and dated scientific progress to the canonical records. EL01 remains at scientific status “Partial progress”; its entry version is now 1.1.
- Updated the site's last-updated date while preserving the first-publication date of 6 October 2026.

## 10 October 2026 — Website sharing QR code

- Added PNG and SVG QR codes with the OPM lettermark, linking directly to the public homepage.
- Added a README preview, download links, and guidance for use in slides, documents, and print.

## 8 October 2026 — Attribution wording correction

- Updated the initiative description to “an independent research initiative” to match the manuscript.
- Preserved the first-publication date of 6 October 2026 and all scientific problem-statement versions.

## v1.0 — 6 October 2026

- Established the canonical first-release set of 10 mechanics problems.
- Integrated Home, Problems, and Vision into the single-page Mechanics Atlas.
- Completed source, mathematics-rendering, internal-navigation, citation-link, mobile, desktop, and print/PDF checks for the release candidate.
- Uses native MathML for static GitHub Pages hosting.
- Added CI validation for the actual published artifact.
- Added explicit scientific-content and software licensing.
- Added a maintenance protocol and durable canonical/progress records.
- Removed stale generated pages and transient drafting artifacts from the publication repository.
